EURUSD Advanced Tradability Zones 2023

Each 15-minute weekday zone is ranked on liquidity, clean movement and target-before-stop performance using calendar 2023. Bright green cells are positive and have a positive 30-minute parent with the same strategy. Amber cells are positive at 15 minutes but lack 30-minute confirmation. The 30-minute aggregation is retained as a confirmation layer. Red zones are negative or historical avoid zones. This is an annual descriptive map, not an OOS validation.
