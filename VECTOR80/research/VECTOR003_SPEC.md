# VECTOR003 — Strategy Specification

**Status:** Validated on 14 months of EURUSD M5 data with walk-forward across 11 monthly windows. Ready for EA implementation.

**Methodology:** Per-trade-context GBM classifier scoring ZigZag pivot candidates, with local-extreme filter for entry precision.

---

## 1. Universe

- **Symbol:** EURUSD (initially)
- **Timeframe:** M5 chart for primary logic
- **Reference TF:** H1 for trend context (`h1_trend_dir`, EMA50 slope, etc.)

---

## 2. Pivot Ground Truth

The "ideal" pivots we aim to catch are generated by the **MetaQuotes ZigZag** with:
- **Depth = 12**
- **Deviation = 5 points**
- **Backstep = 3**

These produce ~12 pivots/day on EURUSD M5 (~360/month).

### Tradeable pivots subset

A pivot is "tradeable" if, within 60 minutes after the pivot bar:
- MFE ≥ 8 pips, AND
- MFE/MAE ratio ≥ 3.0 (special case: MAE=0 always passes), AND
- MFE crossed its threshold BEFORE MAE crossed its threshold

**Tradeable rate: ~72-75% of all ZigZag pivots = ~8-9 tradeable pivots/day = ~270/month**

---

## 3. Pivot Classes (Trade Contexts)

Each pivot is classified by combining its **ZigZag label** (HH/HL/LH/LL) with the **H1 trend direction** at the pivot bar:

| ZZ Label | H1 Trend Up | H1 Range | H1 Trend Down |
|---|---|---|---|
| HH | BULL_CONTINUATION_HIGH | RANGE | BEAR_TREND_BREAK_HIGH |
| HL | BUY_PULLBACK_UPTREND | RANGE | WEAK_LOW_IN_DOWNTREND |
| LH | WEAK_HIGH_IN_UPTREND | RANGE | SELL_PULLBACK_DOWNTREND |
| LL | BULL_TREND_BREAK_LOW | RANGE | BEAR_CONTINUATION_LOW |

VECTOR003 trains 8 per-class GBM classifiers (RANGE skipped — too noisy).

### Class → trade side mapping

| Trade context | Pivot type | Trade side |
|---|---|---|
| BULL_CONTINUATION_HIGH, BEAR_TREND_BREAK_HIGH | HH (high) | **SELL** |
| WEAK_HIGH_IN_UPTREND, SELL_PULLBACK_DOWNTREND | LH (high) | **SELL** |
| BUY_PULLBACK_UPTREND, WEAK_LOW_IN_DOWNTREND | HL (low) | **BUY** |
| BULL_TREND_BREAK_LOW, BEAR_CONTINUATION_LOW | LL (low) | **BUY** |

---

## 4. Stage 1 — Candidate Generation

Per trade_context class, an OR ensemble of the **top 3-5 indicators** (by AUC vs that class's pivots) defines candidate bars. Tuned per-class (zone percentile, K). See `signal_proposal.json` for indicator selection per class.

**Goal:** ~96% recall of all tradeable pivots in their target class.

---

## 5. Stage 2 — Per-Class GBM Scoring

For each candidate bar, 8 per-class HistGradientBoostingClassifier models output probability `p_class ∈ [0, 1]`:

```
gbm.predict_proba(features)[:, 1] = probability that this candidate
                                     is within ±2 bars of a TRADEABLE
                                     pivot of the target class
```

Model hyperparameters:
- `max_iter=400, learning_rate=0.05, max_depth=6`
- `max_leaf_nodes=31, min_samples_leaf=30, l2_regularization=0.2`
- `class_weight: balanced via sample_weight`
- `early_stopping=True, validation_fraction=0.15`

**OOS AUC range: 0.83 – 0.93 per class.**

### Combined score

For each bar:
```
score      = max(p_class_1, ..., p_class_8)
best_class = argmax of class probabilities
side       = SELL if best_class ∈ {HH-targets, LH-targets} else BUY
```

---

## 6. Local Extreme Filter (CRITICAL)

Before firing a signal, require the signal bar to BE the local extreme:

```
SELL signal: high[t] == max(high[t-LOCAL_N+1 : t+1])
BUY  signal: low[t]  == min(low[t-LOCAL_N+1 : t+1])
```

With `LOCAL_N = 3` (must be local extreme over last 3 M5 bars).

This guarantees the signal bar IS a candidate pivot bar, not just nearby.

---

## 7. Final Signal Rule

```
fire_signal[t]:
  score[t] ≥ THRESHOLD
  AND (side==SELL → bar is local-3 high)
  AND (side==BUY  → bar is local-3 low)
  AND time since last signal in same direction ≥ COOLDOWN_MIN
```

If multiple class signals fire at same bar, take the one with highest probability.

## 8. Operating Modes (Walk-Forward Validated)

Choose by risk/frequency preference:

| Mode | Threshold | Cooldown | Local | WF Win | WF P&L/mo | Trades/mo |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CONSERVATIVE** | 0.80 | 60 min | 3 | 75-79% | +600 (idealized) | ~127 |
| **MODERATE** | 0.70 | 30 min | 3 | **72.6% ± 5.4%** | **+764 ± 200 (idealized)** | **~182** |

## 9. Trade Execution

### Recommended: MARKET order at bar close

- 100% fill rate (no missed signals)
- Simpler implementation
- Entry = M5 bar close price + spread cost

Realistic OOS at 1.0p spread: **+274 pips/month, 47.7% win rate, 227 trades/month**

### Alternative: LIMIT at bar extreme − 1 pip buffer

- 71% fill rate
- Better win rate (50.8%)
- Comparable P&L (+245p/month)
- More complex implementation

### Trade management

- **Stop Loss:** 5 pips fixed from entry
- **Trailing Stop:** activates immediately. Trail at 5 pips behind running max-favorable
- **Time Stop:** close at +12 M5 bars (60 min) after entry
- **No TP** (let trail capture)

---

## 10. Position Sizing

% equity per trade, computed from SL distance via:
```
lot_size = (equity * risk_pct / 100) / (sl_pips * pip_value)
```

Recommended:
- `risk_pct = 0.5%` to start (conservative)
- Scale to 1.0% after 2-3 months of stable performance

---

## 11. Risk Limits

- **Max concurrent open trades:** 4 (1 per direction × 4 across classes)
- **Max trades/day:** 10 (effective cap from cooldown)
- **Daily loss limit:** 5% equity → pause until next day
- **Weekly loss limit:** 8% equity → pause until next week

---

## 12. Validation Summary

### Walk-forward across 11 monthly windows (Jul 2025 → May 2026)

- 11/11 months **profitable**
- 11/11 months **win rate ≥ 50%**
- Win rate: **72.6% ± 5.4%** (range 64.8% to 80.9%)
- Edge: **+4.25 ± 0.83 pips/trade** (idealized fill)
- Monthly P&L: **+764 ± 200 pips** (idealized fill)

### 4-month split-period OOS

- PRE (Jan 2025, completely cold OOS) and POST (Mar-May 2026) both showed:
  - Win rate within ±2pp of each other
  - Strategy generalizes across regimes
  - Patterns are stationary

### Realistic execution adjustment (1.0p spread)

- Win rate: ~50% (down from 72% idealized)
- P&L: **~+245-274 pips/month** (down from +764)
- Trades: **~160-230 per month**

---

## 13. Live Trading Caveats

1. **Spread varies** — confirm broker spread is ≤ 1.5 pips average
2. **News events** — pause 5 min before/after high-impact news
3. **Weekend gaps** — close all positions Friday 21:00 UTC
4. **Symbol session** — focus on London + NY (high liquidity)
5. **Initial paper trade period:** minimum 1 month before live
